Visitor Guide
Visitor Safety & Protocols VMS Enterprise Facilities

Welcome to Our Organization!

We are committed to providing a secure, comfortable, and seamless experience for all guests. Review the entry protocols below before arriving at our reception desk.

1. Valid Identification

All visitors must present an official government photo ID at the entrance for verification against their pre-booked pass.

Accepted Documents: National Identity Card (NIC), Passport, or Driving License.

2. Digital Entry Pass

Show your digital visitor pass on your smartphone or printout to the security reception kiosk for instant barcode check-in.

Pass Retrieval: Access your passes anytime in the My Visit Passes tab.

3. Instant Host Alerts

The moment security marks your pass as checked-in, your meeting host receives an automated notification that you have arrived.

Waiting Lounge: Enjoy complimentary refreshments in Lobby 1 while your host arrives.

4. Visiting Hours

Standard administrative & meeting reception hours are Monday through Friday from 08:30 AM to 05:30 PM.

After-Hours Access: Requires prior security clearance authorized by department heads.

4-Step Visitor Journey

How your visit works from pre-booking to departure

1

Pre-Register Pass

Book your visit online through your User Portal. Select your host officer and purpose of visit.

2

Reception Check-In

Arrive at the main entrance lobby. Present your NIC/Passport. Receive your printed visitor badge.

3

Conduct Meeting

Meet your host officer in designated conference areas or office rooms. Badge must be worn visibly.

4

Express Check-Out

Return your visitor lanyard badge at the security turnstiles. Your pass status updates to Completed.

Guest Amenities & Connectivity

  • •
    High-Speed Guest Wi-Fi: Connect to network VMS-Guest-Fiber. Inquire at reception for daily access credentials.
  • •
    Visitor Parking: Dedicated guest parking slots are located in Wing C, complimentary for registered pass holders.
  • •
    Cafeteria & Lounge: Located on Level 1, offering hot beverages, snacks, and quiet seating.
  • •
    Accessibility: Full wheelchair ramps and elevator access available at all main entrances.

Security & Safety Regulations

  • •
    Visible Badge Wear: Your visitor pass badge must remain visibly worn above the waist at all times.
  • •
    Restricted Zones: Access is restricted strictly to your authorized meeting host's department and public lounges.
  • •
    No Unauthorized Photography: Photography or video recording in server rooms, executive suites, and labs is strictly prohibited.
  • •
    Emergency Evacuation: In case of alarm, follow green emergency exit signs and assemble at Point A (Main Courtyard).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Click on any question below to view detailed answers

How early should I arrive before my scheduled appointment?
We recommend arriving 10 to 15 minutes before your scheduled meeting time. This allows sufficient time for parking, security verification, and badge printing at the front desk.
Can I bring personal electronic devices like laptops or tablets?
Yes, personal laptops, tablets, and smartphones are permitted. Security may ask you to declare serial numbers for enterprise laptops at the gate during entry.
What if my host officer is delayed or in an unexpected meeting?
Our front desk receptionist will contact the host's executive secretary or department coordinator immediately. You are welcome to wait comfortably in the Level 1 Visitor Executive Lounge.
How do I cancel or reschedule a pre-registered visit pass?
Navigate to the My Visit Passes section from your sidebar. You can view your pass details or simply pre-register a new pass with your updated arrival date and time.
