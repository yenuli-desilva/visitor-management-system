Help & Guidelines

Everything You Need to Know

Essential operational instructions for system users, receptionists, and organizational staff. Browse guidelines without needing to sign into any dashboard.

1

How to Sign In

Click Sign In in the top navbar or hero section. Enter your credentials. The system administrator accesses the Administrator Command Portal, while registered users/customers enter their personal User Portal.

2

How to Register as a User

Click Register in the top navbar. Provide your Full Name, preferred username, and password (minimum 6 characters). Observe the live password strength meter. Upon successful registration, you can immediately sign in to your personal User Portal.

3

How to Book & Pre-Register a Visit

Inside your personal User Portal, click Book a Visit. Provide your National ID (NIC), contact phone number, designated department, host employee, and purpose. A digital visit pass is generated instantly.

4

How to Check In & Check Out

Check-In: For returning or scheduled guests, click the teal Check-In button in the visitors directory to record a new arrival timestamp.
Check-Out: When a visitor departs, click the red Check-Out button. Confirm in the dialog to stamp their exit time and update status to Checked Out.

5

How to Search & Filter Visitors

The visitors directory includes a live debounced search bar. Simply start typing a guest's name, National ID number, or host employee name. You can also filter the table instantly by visiting department, check-in status (In Premises vs. Checked Out), or visit date.

6

How to Generate Audit Reports

Open the Reports tab from your portal. You can generate a Daily Log Report by selecting any calendar date, or a Monthly Summary Report with department volume graphs. Click Print Report to generate a clean, printer-friendly paper summary.

☆

Strict Portal Separation & Security Governance

The Administrator Portal is strictly restricted to organization supervisors for managing user accounts, editing passwords, blocking unauthorized operators, and audit oversight. Ordinary staff operators operate solely within the Front Desk Reception Portal. Staff users cannot view or access the Admin Command Portal, and administrators have their dedicated management suite.
