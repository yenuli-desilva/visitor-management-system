Important Visiting Protocols & Guidelines

For your convenience and premises security, please keep the following in mind when visiting:

1. Identification Required: Bring your original National Identity Card (NIC), driving license, or passport for verification at the reception gate.
2. Office Hours: General visitor reception is open Monday through Friday from 08:30 to 17:30.
3. Wear Visitor Badge: Wear your visitor badge visibly at all times while inside organizational premises.
