HOST MANAGEMENT

Who Are Visitors Visiting? (Host Officers)

Administrators configure internal staff members and department officers who are eligible to host visitor appointments.

  • Adding New Hosts: Click Host Officers from the top navigation bar or sidebar, then click + Add New Host Officer. Provide their full name, department, title/role, office/room, phone extension, and email.
  • Visitor Selection: When visitors book appointments or register at reception, they select from this official list of Host Officers. Selecting a host automatically populates the department and office room.
  • User Portal Visibility: Registered customers and visitors browsing the User Portal can view available Host Officers with their department badges and directly click "Book Visit to Meet".

1 System Authentication & Access Control

Access login.html using your assigned username and password. Administrative accounts are automatically directed to the Executive Command Center (admin-dashboard.html), while standard customer accounts access their dedicated User Portal (user-dashboard.html).

2 Visitor Registration & Entrance Pass

Click + Register Visitor. Enter the guest's Full Name, NIC (National ID), contact number, purpose of visit, and select the appointed Host Officer. The system provides a live holographic pass preview before saving. Once submitted, the visitor is marked In Premises and issued their digital badge.

4 How to Check In & Check Out Visitors

Check-In: For returning visitors or guests who are currently marked as Checked Out, click the teal Check-In button in the visitors table to log a new arrival timestamp.

Check-Out: When a guest departs, click the red Check-Out button in either the Dashboard or Visitors Directory. Confirm in the dialog. The system logs the departure timestamp and changes the status to Checked Out.

5 How to Search & Filter Visitors

On the Visitors page, use the search box to search dynamically by Name, NIC, or Host Name with automatic 300ms live debouncing. You can also filter by Department, Status (In Premises / Checked Out), and Visit Date.

6 How to Generate Daily & Monthly Reports

Open Reports in the sidebar. Select Daily Report and pick a date to view total visits, checked-in count, checked-out count, and complete visitor details for that day. Switch to Monthly Report to view overall monthly guest volumes and department shares. Click Print Report to generate an official printout or PDF.

Admin Administrator Features

  • User Management: Add new users, edit full names, reset passwords, and assign roles.
  • Block/Unblock Accounts: Prevent unauthorized operators from signing into the portal.
  • Delete Accounts & Visitors: Remove obsolete visitor profiles or user accounts (the system safeguards against accidental self-deletion of your active admin account).
